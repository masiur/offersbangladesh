# 03 — Competitor Analysis (Bangladesh & Global Benchmarks)

To win in Bangladesh, you are **not** just competing against other deal websites—you are competing against **super-apps, banks, telcos, and Facebook groups** for user attention and merchant budgets.

---

## 1. Tier 1: Direct Local Offer & Deal Aggregators (Bangladesh)

| Competitor | What They Do | Strengths | Weaknesses / Gaps We Can Exploit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **OfferTracker BD** | Mobile app aggregating bank card (EBL, BRAC, City, SCB, DBBL) and MFS (bKash, Nagad) discounts by bank, city, and category. | Validates the exact "bank/MFS aggregator" pain point in BD; clean niche focus. | Narrower scope (mostly card/MFS); limited hyperlocal map/SME social-commerce deals; weaker SEO web presence & community verification. |
| **Offerong / OfferBD / DiscountBD-style Portals** | Web directories listing retail, fashion (Aarong, Apex, Bishworang), tech, and seasonal discounts. | Capture Google SEO traffic for "[Brand] offer BD" queries. | Cluttered UI, poor mobile experience, frequently **stale/expired offers**, no personalized "My Wallet" matching, no geo-location map. |
| **Yeamazing** | Online shopping & rewards/cashback platform with partner local brands. | Bridges shopping + cashback in BD. | Small merchant catalog; lacks comprehensive offline/bank/telco/MFS coverage. |
| **Facebook Groups & Pages** *(FoodBank, Deal/Coupon groups, Telegram channels)* | Crowd-posted deals, restaurant platters, Daraz promo codes, flash sales. | Massive reach, real-time, high engagement, zero cost. | **Terrible searchability & structure**—impossible to filter by "My EBL card + Gulshan + Valid Today"; buried under spam, memes, and expired posts. |

| Platform | Offer Ecosystem | Why Users Use Them | Why They CANNOT Solve the Full Problem |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **bKash** (~67% MFS share) | In-app "Offers" tab: merchant cashback, grocery (Shwapno/Meena Bazar), dining, bill pay rewards. | Already installed on almost every phone; instant cashback. | **Walled garden:** Will never show Nagad, Rocket, City Amex, or GPStar deals. Poor filtering/map discovery. |
| **Nagad / Rocket / Upay** | Periodic cashback & campaign offers inside their apps. | Aggressive seasonal cashbacks (Eid, Pohela Boishakh). | Same walled garden problem; users don't open 4 MFS apps to compare which one has the best deal at a store. |
| **Foodpanda (pandapro)** | Delivery vouchers, up to 25% pickup discounts, dine-in discounts at partner restaurants. | Frictionless redemption inside the app; strong subscription lock-in. | Only covers food/grocery/pandamart; ignores clothing, hotels, electronics, and bank card BOGO buffets. |
| **Daraz** | Flash sales, collectible vouchers, Brand Days, bank/MFS checkout discounts. | Dominant e-commerce marketplace in BD. | Only covers Daraz online shopping; zero offline retail/dining coverage. |
| **Pathao (Pathao Points)** | Loyalty points & partner discounts for rides/food. | Built into daily commute/food app. | Narrow partner list; points-gated rather than universal offer discovery. |
| **ShareTrip / GoZayaan** | Travel, flight, and hotel promo codes + bank card discounts. | Great for travel/hotel bookings. | Travel-only vertical. *(Great affiliate partners for us!)* |

## 4. Tier 4: Global & Regional Benchmarks (What to Copy & What to Avoid)

Studying global winners and failures saves years of expensive mistakes:

### 1. **ShopBack** (Singapore / SEA / Global)
- **Model:** Affiliate cashback + ShopBack Pay (offline QR) + Gift Vouchers + Rewards-as-a-Service (RaaS).
- **Lesson for Us:** Pure affiliate cashback works great in mature e-commerce markets (Shopee/Lazada), **but** in Bangladesh, affiliate tracking is immature. Copy their **gamified "Challenges"** and **B2B RaaS** later, not pure cashback on Day 1.

### 2. **Magicpin** (India — Closest Regional Parallel!)
- **Model:** Hyperlocal O2O discovery for offline dining, fashion, grocery, and pharmacy. Users upload receipts/bills or buy discounted vouchers to earn points.
- **Lesson for Us:** Offline retail/dining dominates South Asia (90%+ of commerce is still offline). Copy Magicpin's **hyperlocal neighborhood-first UX** and **SME merchant focus**. Note: receipt OCR verification is clever for offline attribution without POS integration!

### 3. **The Entertainer** (UAE / GCC / Global)
- **Model:** Paid annual subscription for high-value BOGO offers (fine dining, 5-star hotels, resorts, spas). Also sells white-label B2B subscriptions to banks and corporates.
- **Lesson for Us:** Consumers *will* pay for a subscription if 1–2 uses pay back the annual fee (e.g., a ৳999/year pass that saves ৳3,000 on one 5-star buffet or Cox's Bazar resort). Great **Phase 3** monetization layer—especially for B2B corporate/bank gifting.

### 4. **Honey (PayPal) & Rakuten** (US / Europe)
- **Model:** Browser extension auto-applying coupons at checkout + cashback portal.
- **Lesson for Us:** Browser extensions won't move the needle in Bangladesh because **desktop usage is only 11.8%**. Mobile-first (app + mobile web) is the only path in BD.

### 5. **Slickdeals / HotUKDeals / DesiDime** (US / UK / India)
- **Model:** Community-curated deal forums with upvotes, deal alerts, and affiliate links.
- **Lesson for Us:** Community voting ("Hot" / "Expired") is the **cheapest, fastest way to keep data accurate** and build organic SEO authority.

### 6. **Groupon (Cautionary Tale)**
- **What Went Wrong:** Forced merchants into unsustainable 50% discounts + took a 50% cut of the remainder -> merchants lost money, treated Groupon users poorly, and churned.
- **Lesson for Us:** **Never bleed SMEs dry.** Help merchants fill *off-peak capacity* (e.g., Sunday–Wednesday lunch, off-season Cox's Bazar rooms, seasonal clearance stock) with sustainable 10%–25% offers and fair, transparent pricing.

## 5. Competitive Positioning Matrix (Where the White Space Is)

| Capability | Bank/Telco Apps | bKash/Nagad | OfferTracker BD | FB Deal Groups | **Our Platform (Target)** |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Cross-Bank + MFS + Telco Aggregation | No | No | Partial | Unstructured | **Yes (Full)** |
| Personalized "My Wallet" Matching | No | No | Partial | No | **Yes (Core)** |
| Hyperlocal "Near Me" Map Discovery | No | No | Limited | No | **Yes** |
| SME Facebook/Insta Promo Ingestion (AI) | No | No | No | Manual/Messy | **Yes (Moat)** |
| Verified Freshness + Community Voting | No | N/A | Limited | Messy | **Yes** |
| Bilingual (English + Bangla + Banglish) | Partial | Yes | Partial | Yes | **Yes** |
| SEO Web + Native Android/iOS Apps | No | App-only | App-heavy | Walled | **Both** |

### The White Space Summary
Nobody in Bangladesh owns the **neutral, cross-ecosystem "Offer Layer"** that sits *above* all banks, MFS apps, telcos, e-commerce sites, and Facebook SMEs—combining **personalized wallet matching**, **hyperlocal map discovery**, and **AI-verified freshness** across both Google-searchable web and mobile apps.
