# 01 — Brainstorming & Product Vision

## 1. The Core Problem: "Offer Fragmentation" in Bangladesh

In Bangladesh, discounts and cashbacks are everywhere—yet nearly impossible to track when you actually need them.

Imagine a typical middle-class consumer in Dhaka or Chattogram going out for dinner and shopping on a Friday evening. They carry:
- 1–2 **Bank Debit/Credit Cards** (e.g., City Bank Amex, EBL Visa, BRAC Bank)
- 2 **MFS Wallets** (bKash, Nagad)
- 1–2 **Mobile SIMs** (Grameenphone GPStar, Robi Elite, or Banglalink Orange Club)
- 3–4 **Apps** (Foodpanda, Pathao, Daraz, Aarong/Apex loyalty)

Right now, when they walk into a restaurant in Dhanmondi or a clothing store in Bashundhara City, they have **no fast way to answer one simple question**:
> *"Which of my cards, wallets, or SIMs gives me the best discount at this exact place right now?"*

To find out, they would have to:
1. Open the bKash app and scroll through dozens of unsearchable campaign banners.
2. Google "EBL Visa restaurant offers 2026" and download a 14-page poorly formatted PDF.
3. Open MyGP to check GPStar partner lists.
4. Search the restaurant's Facebook page to see if there's a "Flat 20% off" flyer posted 3 days ago.

**Result:** 90% of people don't bother, pay full price, and only find out *afterward* that they could have saved 15%–50%.

## 2. Brainstorming: 5 Distinct Product Angles

A "centralized offers platform" can take very different shapes. Below are the 5 core directions—and how they can be combined into a cohesive product.

### Angle A: The "Wallet-Aware" Offer Engine *(Recommended Core Wedge)*
- **Concept:** Users configure their "Digital Wallet Profile" (which banks/cards, MFS apps, and telco tiers they hold—**without entering card numbers or sensitive data**).
- **Magic Moment:** The app personalizes the entire feed to show *only* offers the user is actually eligible for, and highlights the **Best Deal Stack** (e.g., *"Use EBL Visa for 20% off, or bKash for 10% cashback — EBL saves you ৳400 more"*).
- **Why it wins:** Solves cold-start immediately. Even with zero direct merchant sign-ups, there are already **5,000+ active bank/MFS/telco offers** live across Bangladesh on Day 1.

### Angle B: "Near Me" Hyperlocal Offer Radar (O2O)
- **Concept:** A map-based discovery experience (like Google Maps meets Groupon). Open the app inside Jamuna Future Park, Bashundhara City, Banani 11, or Dhanmondi 27 and see every store/restaurant within 500m running a live promo.
- **Magic Moment:** Geofenced push notifications (opt-in): *"You're near Banani 11 — 6 restaurants nearby have BOGO or 20% off with your cards today."*
- **Why it wins:** High purchase intent (user is already out spending money), strong habit loop for weekend dining and Eid shopping.

### Angle C: The "Bangladesh Deal Pulse" (Community + AI Flyer Radar)
- **Concept:** Think **Slickdeals / HotUKDeals / DesiDime** localized for Bangladesh, supercharged with AI. Users and moderators post flash deals, clearance sales, Daraz/Pickaboo voucher codes, and local shop discounts—and upvote/downvote them ("Hot" vs "Cold" / "Expired").
- **Magic Moment:** Real-time alerts for massive seasonal waves (**Ramadan/Eid-ul-Fitr, Eid-ul-Adha, Pohela Boishakh, 11.11, Winter Clearance, Cox's Bazar off-season hotel deals**).
- **Why it wins:** Self-policing freshness (community flags expired deals fast) and massive organic SEO + viral sharing on WhatsApp/Messenger.

### Angle D: Direct Merchant Voucher & Coupon Marketplace (The Entertainer / Magicpin Model)
- **Concept:** Partner directly with restaurants, salons, hotels, and clothing shops to offer **exclusive app-only discounts** (e.g., "Show QR code in-app for 15% off" or "Buy a ৳1,000 dining voucher for ৳800").
- **Why it wins:** Creates an exclusive moat (offers you *can't* get anywhere else) and direct revenue.
- **Why NOT to do this first:** Requires a large feet-on-street sales team, merchant POS training, and regulatory compliance (escrow) if selling paid vouchers. Best for **Phase 2**.

### Angle E: Cross-Platform Cashback Portal (ShopBack / Rakuten Model)
- **Concept:** Click out from our app to Daraz, Chaldal, ShareTrip, GoZayaan, Booking.com, or Agoda, and earn extra cashback on top of store discounts.
- **Why it wins:** Proven global business model with clear affiliate revenue.
- **Limitation in BD:** Local e-commerce affiliate infrastructure in Bangladesh is immature compared to SEA/US (Daraz has an affiliate program, travel sites like Agoda/Booking/Trip.com pay well, but most local clothing brands don't have affiliate tracking yet).

## 3. Target Verticals (Prioritized for Bangladesh)

Covering "everything" is the long-term destination, not the launch day scope. Here is the priority matrix for Bangladesh:

| Priority | Vertical | Offer Types | Why Prioritize / Defer |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P0 (Launch)** | **Dining & Cafes** | Bank BOGO buffets, 10–25% card/telco discounts, platter deals, MFS cashback | Highest repeat frequency (weekly), urban cardholders love it, massive Facebook promo activity. |
| **P0 (Launch)** | **Clothing & Lifestyle** | Eid/seasonal flat sales (Aarong, Apex, Bata, Lotto, Yellow, Cats Eye, Rise, Sailor, Infinity), card discounts | Highest emotional & seasonal search spikes in BD; 45%+ of card spend is retail/department stores. |
| **P0 (Launch)** | **E-Commerce & Groceries** | Daraz/Chaldal/Shwapno/Meena Bazar/Pickaboo vouchers, bKash/Nagad instant cashback | Mass-market daily utility; drives high repeat traffic and SEO. |
| **P1 (Month 3–6)** | **Hotels, Resorts & Travel** | Cox's Bazar, Sylhet, Sajek, Bandarban resorts; 5-star Dhaka hotels; Air Astra/US-Bangla/ShareTrip/GoZayaan promos | High ticket size (৳5,000–৳50,000+); strong affiliate payouts on travel. |
| **P1 (Month 3–6)** | **Electronics & Gadgets** | 0% EMI bank offers, Star Tech/Ryans/Gadget & Gear/Apple/Samsung cashback & exchange deals | High search volume before big purchases; strong intent. |
| **P2 (Month 6+)** | **Health, Beauty & Wellness** | Diagnostics (Praava, Popular, Labaid card discounts), spas, salons, gyms, pharmacies (Arogga) | Huge hidden bank/telco discount category that people forget to use. |
| **P2 (Month 6+)** | **Entertainment, EdTech & Services** | Star Cineplex, amusement parks, Shikho/10 Minute School, ride-hailing (Pathao/Uber), telecom bundles | Good for student/youth engagement and daily app opens. |

## 4. Product Vision Statement

> **Short-Term (Year 1 — Bangladesh):**
> Become Bangladesh's default **"Check Before You Pay"** companion—the single app and website where any Bangladeshi shopper can instantly see every discount, cashback, and perk available to them across their bank cards, MFS wallets, telco SIMs, and favorite local brands.

> **Medium-Term (Year 2–3 — Ecosystem & Monetization):**
> Evolve from a passive aggregator into an **active O2O commerce & discovery platform** with verified exclusive merchant deals, self-serve SME promotion tools, and affiliate cashback across dining, retail, and travel.

> **Long-Term (Year 3+ — Regional Expansion):**
> Replicate the **Wallet-Aware + AI Social-Commerce Offer Engine** playbook into structurally similar, mobile-first, Facebook/WhatsApp-heavy emerging markets where offer fragmentation is equally severe:
> - **Wave 1 Expansion:** Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Nepal (similar bank/wallet/telco fragmentation, high Facebook commerce).
> - **Wave 2 Expansion:** Indonesia, Philippines, Vietnam, Egypt, or GCC (UAE/Saudi — high card/dining BOGO culture, high monetization potential).

## 5. Key Differentiators (Our Unfair Advantage)

If we just build another list of coupons, we lose. Our differentiation must rest on **4 pillars**:
1. **Personalization by Payment Method ("My Wallet"):** Filter noise down to *your* exact cards, MFS, and SIMs.
2. **Bilingual & Banglish Search:** Users in Bangladesh search *"buffet offer dhanmondi"*, *"bkash cashback shwapno"*, *"eid discount aarong"*, and Bengali script (*"ডিসকাউন্ট"*, *"অফার"*). Native Banglish + Bangla search is a huge edge.
3. **AI-Powered Flyer-to-Structured-Data Pipeline:** Automatically turning messy Facebook/Instagram promo images into clean, searchable, geo-tagged, expiry-tracked offers.
4. **Trust & Freshness Score:** Every offer shows a live **"Last Verified"** timestamp, source proof (link/screenshot), exact T&Cs (minimum spend, max cap, valid days), and community "Works / Expired" voting.
