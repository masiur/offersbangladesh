## 1. Macro Digital & Payments Landscape (2025–2026 Data)

Bangladesh is no longer an early-stage digital market—it is a **massive mobile-first digital economy** with rapidly maturing digital payments.

### Key Takeaways for Product Strategy
1. **Android & Mobile Web First:** With only 11.8% PC usage and 122M+ mobile internet users, desktop is secondary. Your web experience must be lightning-fast on 4G Android devices, and Android app will drive 80%+ of mobile downloads (iOS is smaller by volume, ~8–12%, but **disproportionately high-income credit card holders** in Dhaka/Chattogram).
2. **Cards + MFS = Massive Addressable Base:** Even if you only target Bangladesh's **2.81M credit card holders** and **9.27M prepaid card holders**, that's over 12M high-spending digital consumers—before even counting 40.8M debit cardholders and 100M+ active MFS users.
3. **Retail & Dining Dominate Card Spend:** Bangladesh Bank data shows **45%–48% of domestic credit card spend** goes to department stores, retail outlets, clothing, and dining—the exact verticals where bank and merchant offers thrive.
4. **Decentralization Beyond Dhaka:** Semi-urban and district towns (Sylhet, Chattogram, Rajshahi, Khulna, Cumilla, Bogura) are driving the fastest e-commerce and MFS growth. Launching in Dhaka first makes sense for offline dining/retail density, while online/MFS/national brand offers serve the entire country immediately.

## 2. Market Sizing (TAM / SAM / SOM — Bangladesh)

### User Base Sizing
- **TAM (Total Addressable Market):** ~65–75 Million urban & semi-urban smartphone users in Bangladesh who actively shop, dine, or use MFS/cards.
- **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market):** ~15–20 Million digitally active urban consumers (Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet, Khulna, Rajshahi, Gazipur, Narayanganj) who hold a bank card, active MFS wallet, or telco loyalty tier and regularly look for deals.
- **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market — Year 1–2):** **250,000 – 1,000,000 MAU** (1.5%–5% of SAM), heavily concentrated in Dhaka & Chattogram metro areas.

### Revenue Market Sizing (Conservative Bangladesh B2B/Affiliate Potential)
- **Digital Ad & Promotional Spend in BD:** Brands, restaurants, fashion houses, banks, and e-commerce players spend hundreds of millions of USD annually on Facebook/Google ads, SMS blasts, and seasonal Eid/Ramadan campaigns.
- **Target Revenue SOM (Year 2–3):**
  - If we reach **500K MAU** with an ARPU of just **৳15–৳30/month ($0.12–$0.25)** blended across sponsored listings, affiliate commissions, and brand campaigns -> **৳75 Lakh – ৳1.5 Crore/month (~$60K–$125K/month or $720K–$1.5M ARR)**.
  - *Note:* Achieving high ARPU in Bangladesh is hard; profitability depends on keeping operational & acquisition costs lean.

## 3. Core User Personas

### Persona 1: The Urban Professional ("Card & Lifestyle Maximizer")
- **Profile:** Age 25–40, Dhaka/Chattogram, corporate job / tech / banker / entrepreneur. Holds 1–3 credit cards (City Amex, EBL, BRAC, SCB), bKash, GPStar Gold/Platinum.
- **Behavior:** Dines out 2–4x/month, shops at Aarong/Apex/Yellow/Lotto, travels to Cox's Bazar/Sylhet/abroad 2x/year.
- **Pain Point:** Knows their cards have great BOGO buffets and 15–20% dining/hotel discounts, but forgets which card works where.
- **Value to Us:** **Highest monetization value** (banks, 5-star hotels, travel OTAs, and premium brands pay well to reach them).

### Persona 2: The University Student / Young Value Hunter
- **Profile:** Age 18–25, NSU/BRACU/DU/BUET/CUET/private & public universities. Uses bKash/Nagad, Robi/GP/BL, debit/prepaid card, Foodpanda, Daraz.
- **Behavior:** Hyper price-sensitive, hangs out at Dhanmondi/Mirpur/Uttara/Banani cafes, hunts for platter deals, promo codes, and BOGO food offers.
- **Pain Point:** Spends 30 minutes scrolling Facebook food groups ("FoodBank" etc.) to find affordable group deals.
- **Value to Us:** **Highest viral growth & UGC contributors** (they share deals on Messenger/WhatsApp groups and verify offers).

### Persona 3: The Household / Family Shopper
- **Profile:** Age 30–50, manages monthly groceries (Shwapno, Meena Bazar, Chaldal, Unimart), kids' clothing, pharmacy, and massive Eid/festival shopping.
- **Behavior:** Uses bKash/Nagad cashback, debit cards, supermarket loyalty cards.
- **Pain Point:** Hard to compare whether Shwapno + bKash or Chaldal + Nagad or Unimart + EBL is cheaper this week.
- **Value to Us:** High repeat retention (weekly grocery & monthly essentials).

### Persona 4: The SME Merchant (Supply Side — Restaurant / Boutique / Resort)
- **Profile:** Cafe owner in Mirpur, clothing boutique in Banani/Uttara, or resort manager in Sylhet/Cox's Bazar.
- **Behavior:** Spends ৳15,000–৳100,000/month boosting Facebook posts with terrible ROI and fake/irrelevant comments ("Price?", "Inbox").
- **Pain Point:** Needs high-intent local foot traffic, especially on slow weekdays (Sun–Wed).
- **Value to Us:** **Primary B2B revenue source** once we have proven local traffic.

## 4. Idea Validation Playbook (Before Building Full Apps)

**Golden Rule:** Do **not** spend 6 months and ৳15–25 Lakh building iOS + Android + Web + Admin Panel before validating demand and retention. Run a **4-Week Lean Validation Sprint** costing < ৳30,000 ($250).

### Week 1: Concierge / Manual Dataset + Landing Page
- Manually curate the **top 300 active offers** in Dhaka (Top 5 banks: City, EBL, BRAC, SCB, DBBL + bKash + Nagad + GPStar + top 50 restaurants/clothing brands).
- Put them into a simple, fast **Next.js / Airtable / Notion-backed web app** (or lightweight PWA) with **"Select Your Cards/MFS"** filtering + Area filter (Dhanmondi, Gulshan, Banani, Uttara, Mirpur).
- Add a "Save / Alert Me" email or WhatsApp/Telegram bot signup.

### Week 2: Targeted Distribution Test
- Post organically in Bangladeshi communities (Reddit `r/bangladesh`, Facebook food/tech/finance groups, LinkedIn Dhaka corporate circles).
- Run ৳10,000–৳15,000 ($85–$125) in hyper-targeted Meta ads in Dhaka targeting:
  - Audience A: Credit card holders & frequent diners (Gulshan/Banani/Dhanmondi/Uttara).
  - Audience B: University students (platter & café deals).
- Track **CTR, Cost per Click, Filter Usage, and "Outbound Click / Reveal Deal" rate**.

### Week 3: Retention & Willingness-to-Engage Check
- Send 1 weekly curated digest (WhatsApp Channel / Telegram / Email): *"Top 10 Weekend Deals for Your Cards in Dhaka"*.
- Interview 20–30 active users (10-min calls or Google Form):
  - *Did you actually redeem an offer you found here?*
  - *Which offer was expired or inaccurate?*
  - *Would you install a mobile app for this, or only use Google/Web?*

### Week 4: Merchant Demand Test (B2B Validation)
- Cold-approach 20 restaurants/clothing shops in Dhanmondi or Banani.
- Pitch: *"We have X,000 Dhaka shoppers checking offers daily. Listing your regular offer is free; would you pay ৳2,000–৳5,000/week for a 'Featured Top Deal' slot or push notification to users nearby?"*
- See how many say yes or ask for a pilot.

### Go / Pivot / Kill Thresholds

| Metric | **GO (Build MVP)** | **PIVOT (Refine Wedge)** | **KILL / RETHINK** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meta Ad CTR** | > 2.5% | 1.0% – 2.5% | < 1.0% |
| **Filter / Wallet Setup Rate** | > 35% of visitors | 15% – 35% | < 15% |
| **Day-7 Return Rate (Web/Channel)** | > 20% | 8% – 20% | < 8% |
| **Verified Redemptions (Self-reported)** | 20+ in 2 weeks | 5 – 19 | < 5 |
| **Merchant Interest (out of 20 pitched)** | 5+ willing to list / 2+ willing to pay | 3–4 free listings, 0 paid | < 2 interested |
